Department of Computer Applications

The Department of Computer Applications provides students with a strong foundation in computer science, software development, emerging technologies and professional skills.

The department encourages students to participate in technical activities, projects, seminars, workshops and other academic programmes.

Vision of the Department

To become a Centre of excellence in research, education, development and consultancy in the field of Computer Applications.

Mission of the Department

M1: Provide a strong theoretical and practical competence in computer application.

M2: Provide students with necessary skills to become successful professionals, responsible citizens and life-long learners.

M3: Develop technical solutions in the field of Information Technology for the society and Industry.

Program Educational Objective

PEO 1: Establish career in software industry, academia or launch startup.

PEO 2: Pursue continuous learning through higher studies and research in the field of Computer Applications and other allied streams.

PEO 3: Establish themselves as technical solution architects by the application of Information Technology for the benefit of common man and society.

Program Specific Outcomes (PSOs)

PSO 1: Ability to apply the skill and knowledge in designing, implementing and testing of software systems for practical purpose.

PSO 2: Pursuing continuous learning through higher studies and research in the field of Computer Application and other allied streams.

PEO 3: Enabling to carry out a professional and proactive approach, good interpersonal skills towards projects/multidisciplinary research activities.
